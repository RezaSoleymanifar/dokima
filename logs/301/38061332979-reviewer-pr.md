- No 301.1 test covers that case, so they all pass while the criterion is only partly met.
- The worker did this on purpose: an older test, `test_each_starting_pack_lists_exactly_the_open_raises_for_that_agent` (criterion 300.2), expects exactly `[R3]`, and 301.1 makes that expectation wrong. The worker said so openly in its hand-back.
- The fix:
  1. The planner changes that older test to expect both `R3` and `RW1`, with a reason you can see.
  2. The planner adds a 301.1 test for this case.
  3. The worker then drops the `not (done and for_planner(h))` condition.
