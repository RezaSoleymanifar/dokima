The non-functional requirement now says Dokima never switches the queue on or changes it.
- **Story 4 (autopilot and Approve):** your Approve on a Dokima pull request adds it to the merge queue by itself where main has one. Without a queue, an Approve still starts nothing.

**Other changes:**
- The questions are gone, since you answered both.

**The 5 stories, unchanged otherwise:**
1. The manifest declares main's merge queue, and the Setup issue reports a missing one as above.
2. With a queue on, `uptodate` stops bringing branches up to date but still finds clashes. Without one, it works as today. Depends on 1.
3. The queue's own test runs make `card.yml` and `board.yml` change nothing, and never fail.
4. Autopilot's line says a pull request joined the queue, and the issue says again when it merges or why the queue removed it. Your Approve adds it to the queue. Depends on 1.
5. AGENTS.md describes the merge queue. Depends on 1 to 4.
