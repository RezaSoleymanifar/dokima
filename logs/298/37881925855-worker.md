What the module does, by criterion:
- **298.1 (kinds):** `KINDS` holds only question, blocker and issue. Any other kind is rejected, naming the bad kind and the three allowed ones. Labels are never read, so two raises that differ only in label are checked and routed the same.
- **298.2 (who raises to whom):** `TABLE` is the planner to the owner, the worker to the planner, and the reviewer to the planner, the worker or the owner. A question or blocker that names no one, or someone outside the table, is rejected, naming who raised it, its kind and its target. An issue sent to anyone is also rejected. `sent_to` sends a worker's raise to the planner to the reviewer first.
- **298.3 (stamping):** `stamp` copies each raise, sets who raised it from the agent that ran, and gives it an ID such as `W3` that isn't already on the issue. If the agent writes `raised_by` or `id` itself, the raise is rejected, naming the field.
- **298.4 (answers):** `check_answers` rejects an answer that isn't an object, gives no ID or an ID no open raise has, says anything but done or disagree, or gives no reason. It names the bad one by its place ("answer 2"). It also names, by ID, every raise sent to that agent that was left unanswered.
- **298.5 (one place):** `KINDS` is a tuple and `TABLE` is a read-only mapping of tuples, so neither can be changed while running.

Three things for the reviewer to weigh:
- **Extra check:** a raise carrying any field besides kind, to, label, text, evidence, or the two stamped fields is rejected. The criteria don't ask for this directly; I added it so a hand-back can't carry its own kinds, table or route (298.5).
- **Bad answers still count:** a malformed answer that does name a real open raise counts as answering it. The turn is still rejected for the bad answer, but the raise isn't also reported as unanswered.
- **Raises not sent to the agent:** an answer is accepted if it names any open raise, even one that wasn't sent to that agent. The plan reviewer left that case for #300.
