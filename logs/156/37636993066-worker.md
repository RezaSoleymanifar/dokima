**Agent:** A proposed split is now refused unless every story is complete and its dependencies make sense. The full suite passes (168 tests), but only once I unset two environment variables this machine sets; details below.

**Cause:** in `dokima/planner.py`, `from_kind` only checked that a feature has 2 to 5 stories. A story with no title, criteria without a source, or a story depending on itself all got through to you.

**Change:** I added one function, `check_stories`, in `dokima/planner.py`, called from the feature branch of `from_kind`. It's the only file I changed. Each refusal names the story counting from 1, as the split's card does ("story 2"):
- **156.1:** a story is refused if:
  - its title or user story is missing or blank;
  - its acceptance criteria are missing, empty or not a list;
  - its `depends_on` is missing or not a list (an empty list means no dependencies and passes);
  - any of its criteria lacks its text or source.

  The reason names the field, for example "story 2 has no title" or "story 2: acceptance criterion 2 has no source".
- **156.2:** a dependency is refused if it isn't a number of one of the split's stories, or if it points at the story itself. A loop is refused naming only the stories in it ("stories wait on each other in a loop: story 2, story 3"). Chains, diamonds and dependencies on later stories pass.
- **156.3:** a story or criterion that isn't an object is refused with a reason naming the story, not a crash. That means `rejected.txt` still gets written, so the issue says why.

**Test environment:** this machine sets `PYTHONPATH=/tmp/runtime` and `PYTHONSAFEPATH=1`. With those set, the two tests that run `python3 -m dokima.planner check` as a separate process pick up the runtime's copy of Dokima instead of this branch's code, and they fail. With both unset, as on a plain checkout, all 168 tests pass. I noted this in `work.json` too.
