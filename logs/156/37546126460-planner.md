**User story:** a proposed split reaches the owner only when every story is complete and its dependencies point at the split's own stories, with no loops.

**Acceptance criteria**
- **156.1** A story is rejected when its title, user story, acceptance criteria or `depends_on` list is missing, empty or the wrong type. The reason names the story, counting from 1 the way the split's card does ("story 2"), and the field. A complete split is shown to the owner exactly as handed back.
- **156.2** A story is rejected when it depends on itself, on a story the split doesn't have, or on other stories in a loop. A story the split doesn't have includes an index past the end, a negative one (Python would quietly read it as the last story) and a string or null. The reason names a story at fault and no innocent one. Chains, diamonds and dependencies on later stories pass.
- **156.3** (non-functional, fail closed) A story that isn't an object at all is rejected with a reason, not a crash. Today a crash writes no `rejected.txt`, so the issue would never say why.
